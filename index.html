    San Francisco Cityfront

    Folkboat Racing Sim

    Race Folkboats off a real start line with real current. Every foul pauses the race and replays it from overhead with the rule that applies.

    Mode
    Course
    Wind (westerly)
    The sea breeze for the race day and time, from five years of NOAA wind records at Crissy Field. Puffs and lulls drift across the course either way.
    Current
    Ebb runs west along the Cityfront, toward the Gate; flood runs east. Both are weaker close to shore.
    Sea
    Chop from the wind over the water and the open water upwind; an ebb against the westerly makes it bigger and steeper. Ocean swell from the San Francisco Bar buoy's records for the month, dying away inside the Gate. Chop slows you most upwind, so foot a little to keep her going.
    Crew
    Your weight and your crew's goes into the physics: displacement, how far out they can hike (taller reaches further), and where the weight sits fore and aft. Fewer hands means slower pole sets and line handling. Folkboats usually race with three.
    Ship traffic
    Container ships and tankers in the channel, the Sausalito and Alcatraz ferries, bay-cruise boats and the Coast Guard, on their usual tracks. The racing rules don't apply between you and them; the collision regulations do. Their wakes hit you as a burst of steep waves.

    Penalty for a foul
    The Racing Rules default is two turns (Rule 44.1). Some sailing instructions change it to one; check yours. Crossing the Seaweed Soup line mid-race is always two turns.
    Training aids
    The wind-angle dial (true and apparent wind around a boat outline) doesn't exist on a real boat; the windex and the telltales do. It's hidden by default.
    SteerClick the water (or press T) to take the tiller. While you hold it, the cursor's left–right position is the tiller handle: the dashed centre line is straight ahead; slide right of it to push the tiller to starboard (the boat turns to port), left to turn to starboard. Further from the centre, harder over. Click again, T or Esc lets go, and the tiller springs back to centre. You can also hold A / D to turn to port / starboard. Tack and gybe by steering through the wind. LuffHold S to ease the sails and slow down. This is how you burn time before the gun. TrimThe crew trims the sails unless you switch Trim to "you". Then Trim/Ease, or Z/X for the main and , / . for the jib. Watch the telltales: windward ones lifting means trim in or bear away; leeward ones drooping means ease or head up. HailHail button or H. Near the shore, close-hauled, with a boat to windward stopping you tacking: "Room to tack!" and they must tack (Rule 20). On starboard with a port boat coming at you: "Starboard!" If another boat hails you for room to tack, tack right away. LookArrow keys (or Q / E / R / F), or drag the view on a touch screen. Looking never steers the boat. W or double-tap looks ahead again.
