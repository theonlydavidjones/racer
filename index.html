            How to sail her ▸
            SteerClick the water (or press T) to take the tiller. While you hold it, the cursor's left–right position is the tiller handle: the dashed centre line is straight ahead; slide right of it to push the tiller to starboard (the boat turns to port), left to turn to starboard. Further from the centre, harder over. Click again, T or Esc lets go, and the tiller springs back to centre. You can also hold A / D to turn to port / starboard. Tack and gybe by steering through the wind. LuffHold S to ease the sails and slow down. This is how you burn time before the gun. TrimThe crew trims the sails unless you switch Trim to "you". Then Trim/Ease, or Z/X for the main and , / . for the jib. Watch the telltales: windward ones lifting means trim in or bear away; leeward ones drooping means ease or head up. HailThe Hail tab on the sail controls band (it shows when there's a hail to make) or H. Near the shore, close-hauled, with a boat to windward stopping you tacking: "Room to tack!" and they must tack (Rule 20). On starboard with a port boat coming at you: "Starboard!" If another boat hails you for room to tack, tack right away. LookArrow keys (or Q / E / R / F), or drag the view on a touch screen. Looking never steers the boat. W or double-tap looks ahead again.

            Retire to use her motor?

            A boat racing may use only the wind and the water (RRS 42.1), and not an engine even to get clear after hitting something, unless the sailing instructions allow it (42.3(h), 42.3(i)). Starting the motor retires her: scored RET (RRS A5.1). Then it's hers to use; keep clear of the boats still racing.

            Retire and head home?

            She'll be scored RET, retired (RRS A5.1), and she's no longer racing: keep clear of the boats still racing (RRS 23.1). Her home slip becomes the destination for the navigator and the tiller pilot.
