# Josephine: what the owner has confirmed

Facts about the boat's look that the owner has stated or confirmed. Check new drawing work against this list before
showing it, and add to it whenever the owner confirms something. One line each; note the photo where there is one.

## Hull
- The bow's profile, from the stemhead to the bottom of the keel's leading edge, is one long, flat, gentle S: no straight
  stem, no corner just below the waterline. (Haul-out photos; the lines diagrams.)
- The bow is slim below the water; above the water its topsides are rounded, not flat.
- Clinker planks keep nearly their width end to end, tapering only slightly; they don't come to a point at the bow.
- The stem is straight-raked above the water (not convex).

## Deck and rails
- One toerail, continuous and unbroken along the whole deck edge. No second rail or rubbing strake on the topsides.
- The hiking rail (where the crew sit to hike out) lies inboard of the toerail, alongside the cockpit, the cockpit's
  length (cabin's after end to the coaming's after end). Not alongside the cabin.
- The coaming doesn't change shape at the cabin's after end.

## Cabin
- The cabin roof is a slice of a cone lying on its side, narrowing toward the bow.
- The cabin's whole after wall (the bulkhead with the companionway) is a vertical plane, not slanted. (A slant seen in the
  profile photo was an illusion of its angle.)
- The cabin top rises toward its after end (profile photo).
- The cabin front rounds down to the deck.
- The grab rails run most of the cabin top's length, curved to follow the roof (which slants down forward), about
  halfway between the hatch and the roof's edge.
- The jib car tracks are H-shaped, on the cabin top between the grab rail and the roof's edge; cross-bar about 5 in,
  tracks at most 7 in; the car's forward edge level with the grab rail's forward end. (Photo looking aft from the mast.)
- No T-bar or other fitting on the cabin top besides these.
- The cabin may be dark inside by day. Cushions are brown. The mast is keel-stepped. The lantern stays mounted, dark when unlit.
- The companionway hatch slides forward to open.

## Cockpit
- The lazarettes are solid lockers, not benches.
- The jib winches stand on the side deck about a third of the way along the cockpit, with room for a crew to sit on the
  rail forward of each and another (a little less room) aft of it; their tops about flush with the coaming.
- No post under the thwart (that was the boom crutch, only for stowing). The thwart's underside is curved, about 6 in
  deep at the middle tapering to 4 in at the ends. No slanted panels below it.
- Nothing pokes up through the after deck.
