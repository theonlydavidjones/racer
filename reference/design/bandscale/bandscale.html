Mainsheet: boom angle against sheet eased

The first 6 in of sheet off two-blocked swing the boom about 5° (0.8° per inch). Past 4 ft, each inch gives only about 0.15°. The first 10° of boom takes 6% of the rope to the shroud stop at 70° (Rigging). That's why on a true-rope scale every upwind trim sits in the first few percent of the track.
