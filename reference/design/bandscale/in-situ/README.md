# The middle way, in situ

The owner's "middle way" built into the real sail band (branch `design/band-rope-ticks`, not to be merged), shot from the
live game at 1440×900, 2× pixels, 12 kt, slack water, the sim's crew trimming, each after a minute of race time:

- `1-close-hauled.png`: 39° true, after a minute of race time; boom 8.6°, the traveller up to windward, 4 in out.
- `2-beam-reach.png`: 88° true; boom 36°, 165 in out.
- `3-run.png`: 165° true, wing-on-wing with the pole set; the boom at the sim's stop (85°: the shroud would stop it near
  70°, and Deep Physics is moving that stop), all 493 in out.
- `4-band-close-up.png`: the band alone, close-hauled.

What's drawn: today's marks unchanged; faint hairline ticks of rope on each line (even for the tension lines; every foot
on the working jib sheet, by its lead; the backstay drawn evenly and flagged in its help); under the mainsheet track,
where so many inches of sheet eased put the boom (12, 48 and 192 in labelled), and a live "in out" readout. The help
text (the Coach card, on hover) says what the ticks are. `capture.cjs` takes the shots against that branch's dev server: it holds each heading on the tiller pilot (its panel shows, bottom left) and steps the race a minute of race time (headless frames are too slow to sail it in wall time; the first set of shots, taken in wall time, had barely 10 s of sailing and the crew still trimming).
