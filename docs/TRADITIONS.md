on two things: how central it is to a member's life, and how honestly a sim can portray it. Each is marked **sourced**
(with the link) or **lore** (widely told, but no source found yet). Lore stays out of the sim until it's sourced, or
until the owner confirms it from the fleet.

## Details and sources

1. **The season championship and the Hall of Fame.** The SF Bay Folkboat fleet has named a season champion every year
   since 1958–59, skipper and boat, with no racing in 2020. Some skippers and boats have won it many times. The fleet
   prospered on the Bay from 1950 to 1970, and the first fibreglass Folkboat was molded from a wooden Bay boat in 1975.
   The Hall of Fame page jokes that you "may be quizzed at the bar after the race".
   - Sources: https://sfbayfolkboats.org/hall-of-fame/ and https://sfbayfolkboats.org/history/.
   - In the sim: a club Hall of Fame with every season's champion and boat, your own name added when you win. Real
     names stay out of the sim's fleet without permission.
2. **The season, club to club.** The 2026 schedule:
   - StFYC Spring Fest
   - CYC Knox
   - SYC Classic Boat
   - StFYC Woodies
   - BVBC Plastic Classic
   - Balena Bay
   - SFYC Fall One Design
   - GGYC Singlehanded
   - StFYC Fall Invitational
   - RYC Great Pumpkin

   Most are 6 races. Source: https://sfbayfolkboats.org/racing/.
   - In the sim: Seasons built on the real calendar, each regatta at its real club.
3. **The International Regatta.** Biennial since 1977, with Danish crews among the winners. The 2023 edition was at the
   Corinthian, Tiburon; there was none in 2025. Source: https://sfbayfolkboats.org/intl-regatta/.
   - In the sim: the season's crown event, with visiting boats.
4. **The Wednesday Night Series.** A separate award from the season championship, first to third. Source:
   https://sfbayfolkboats.org/racing/.
   - In the sim: short evening races in the summer westerly, at sunset.
5. **Calling it off in a blow.** By the fleet's practice, a vote on canceling at 23 kt (owner). The fleet's written,
   proposed protocol says cancel above 25 kt, decided by the race chair and texted to the fleet. Source:
   https://sfbayfolkboats.org/racing/. Built: the committee calls racing off at 23 kt.
6. **Burgees and flags.**
   - Sourced:
     - The burgee flies at the masthead, or from the starboard spreader, star up.
     - The ensign flies at the stern, or high on the aftermost sail's leech when under way.
     - Colors are made at 0800 and struck at sunset.
     - By custom the ensign isn't flown while racing.
   - Sources: CCA, https://cruisingclub.org/article/cca-flag-and-burgee-etiquette; USPS,
     https://usps.org/national/fecom/faq/flag/gaffpole.html; Good Old Boat; Manhattan YC, https://myc.org/flag-etiquette/.
     These were read through search summaries; the CCA and USPS hosts are blocked here.
   - The RRS themselves don't limit flags while racing, beyond advertising.
   - On the SF Folkboats (the owner, 13 Nov 2026): the burgee flies on the starboard side, at the jumper strut, and is
     struck for racing. Built that way.
   - Built: the ensign keeps its hours (0800 to sunset, and entering or leaving harbor after dark: USPS, Seattle YC;
     src/boat/colours.ts).
   - **Officers' flags** (CCA, https://cruisingclub.org/article/cca-flag-and-burgee-etiquette, 29 March 2024):
     - A commodore's, vice commodore's or rear commodore's flag flies day and night at the masthead of a sloop, in
       place of the burgee, on the officer's own boat.
     - A guest officer flies it at the starboard spreader, with the owner's permission.
     - A past officer flies it at the starboard spreader at club rendezvous.
     - A private signal goes on the main's leech, about two-thirds of the way up from the clew, or at the masthead in
       place of the burgee.
     - The flags' designs aren't described.
   - **Dressing ship** (CCA, same page):
     - Allowed "when not underway (or when underway in a marine parade)", weather permitting, from morning to evening
       colors.
     - A marine parade is what Opening Day's parade is.
     - Any order of signal flags will do. The suggested order from the bow is: AB2, UJ1, KE3, GH6, IV5, FL4, DM7, P O
       3rd repeater, R N 1st repeater, S T 0, C X 9, W Q 8, Z Y 2nd repeater, answering pennant. The first two run
       down to the water at the bow, the last two to the transom or boom.
     - The Union Jack may fly at the bow on Sundays, holidays, or when dressed, only when not under way.
   - Not yet sourced: dipping the ensign, or saluting. The CCA page doesn't cover it, and the USPS pages couldn't be
     reached.
   - In the sim: the burgee at the masthead, the ensign struck at the preparatory signal (both partly in), and colors
     at the club.
7. **The committee and protests.**
   - Sourced:
     - Flag X with one sound for a boat over the line at her start (RRS 29.1).
     - To protest, hail "Protest" and fly a red flag; boats under 6 m need only hail (RRS 61.1(a)). A Folkboat is
       7.64 m, so she flies the flag.
   - Sources: https://www.racingrulesofsailing.org/posts/1838-hailing-protest and the US Sailing protest hearing card.
   - Lore: the gun or horn for the winner (the RRS require no finish signal; it's a sailing-instructions custom), and
     thanking the committee.
   - In the sim: largely built. The hearing room is the protests item.
8. **Perpetual trophies.**
   - GGYC's Manuel Fagundes Seaweed Soup Regatta, a monthly winter series. One account says its perpetual retired to
     the club in 2013 after 42 years.
   - StFYC's St. Francis Perpetual Trophy, first awarded in 1964 at the Big Boat Series.
   - Sources: https://www.sail-world.com/-38627 and https://www.sailingscuttlebutt.com/?p=81156 (secondary).
   - The Folkboat fleet's own:
     - **The Contender's Cup:** winners listed by year, 1981–2025, on https://sfbayfolkboats.org/hall-of-fame/. What
       it's awarded for isn't stated; the winners suggest a newer-skipper award (unconfirmed).
     - **The Rick Wolf trophy** for the Wednesday evening series, named for USA 76's owner from 1956. One secondary
       source, a 2009 awards-dinner blog post seen only as a search snippet; unconfirmed.
     - **The San Francisco International Cup:** the biennial International Regatta, so named on
       https://sfbayfolkboats.org/the-class/.
     - Not found: names for the season championship's trophy, the Singlehanded race's, or any Folkboat perpetuals at
       the Woodies, the Knox or the Great Pumpkin. The owner may find out.
   - **Placeholders** (owner, 13 November): where no real name is found, the sim uses a placeholder name, marked as a
     placeholder in this doc and in the code, so it's never shown as a real trophy's history.
   - In the sim: each trophy with every winner's name, year after year.
9. **Opening Day on the Bay.** Sponsored by PICYA, open to all boaters, usually a Sunday in late April (26 April in
   2026). The Corinthian hosts the Blessing of the Fleet in Raccoon Strait in the morning. The parade runs from off
   StFYC along the Cityfront to Pier 39 from noon. Sources: Modern Sailing's skipper instructions and event pages,
   secondary; picya.org not yet checked.
   - In the sim: a cruising event day, its parade boats dressed overall (a marine parade is where dressing ship is done
     under way).
10. **The Singlehanded race.** GGYC's one-race Singlehanded Regatta in October, dating to 1969 and revived in 2020.
    Sources: https://sfbayfolkboats.org/racing/; Latitude 38, https://www.latitude38.com/?p=44473 (secondary).
    - In the sim: racing alone, as the Folkboat is meant to be sailed.
11. **Wooden and classic boats.** The Master Mariners Benevolent Association dates to 1867. Its Memorial Day regatta is
    for classic and wooden boats, and its Wooden Boat Show is at the Corinthian. The fleet also sails StFYC's Woodies
    and SYC's Classic Boat regatta.
    - Sources: https://www.latitude38.com/?p=106098;
      https://www.cruisingworld.com/how/master-mariners-wooden-boat-show-bit-bay-history/.
    - In the sim: wooden Folkboats and their care (varnish, condition) as part of the boat, and a classic-boat regatta.
12. **Finding crew.** In the fleet, crew come by word of mouth, by people reaching out directly, and through some
    websites and apps (owner). One such site: Latitude 38's free Crew List, which matches skippers and crew, with Crew
    List parties in Sausalito (https://latitude38.com/crew-list).
    - In the sim: the Yacht Club's crew trading (AI members only, for now).
13. **After the race** (owner): always drinks. Depending on the race, often more, in rising order:
    - drinks;
    - drinks and dinner;
    - drinks, dinner and results;
    - drinks, dinner, results and awards.

    The Hall of Fame jokes you "may be quizzed at the bar after the race". Still lore: the pickle dish, the winner
    buying a round, rookie duties.
    - In the sim: after each race, the club's evening at the right level for the race, ending in the results and,
      where there are any, the awards.
14. **Race-specific customs: the Plastic Classic.**
    - Sourced:
      - Bay View Boat Club's regatta, on the third Saturday in July (18 July 2026), one race on the fleet's calendar
        (https://sfbayfolkboats.org/racing/; a 2010 notice).
      - For vintage fiberglass boats only, with a concours d'élégance judged at BVBC's dock that non-racing plastic
        boats are invited to.
      - Run since about 1985, from a 1984 bar conversation.
      - 71 boats in 9 divisions over a 10.7 nm course in 2014; Folkboats race in its handicap divisions (secondary:
        the sail-world and norcalsailing reports, seen through search summaries).
    - The fleet's custom (owner): a boat near the club is both the committee boat and a rounding mark, and its crew of
      women famously flash the boats as they round.
    - In the sim (owner's decision, 13 November, replacing the earlier "nothing explicit"): as in real life, "this is a
      true to life sim." The committee boat's crew flash the fleet as boats round: adult women, topless only, nothing
      sexual, drawn in the crew figures' own stylized style. Whether it sits behind a mature-content setting in
      Settings, and that setting's default, is pending the owner's answer. Before any public release, the host club's
      permission covers this as well as its name.
    - Not yet sourced: the course and marks (norcalsailing.com and sail-world.com are blocked here).
15. **Dressing ship.** Kept as reference (item 6 has the CCA's practice); the owner hasn't seen it done in the fleet, so
    it's ranked low.
