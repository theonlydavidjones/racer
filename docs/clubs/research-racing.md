# The racing clubs: what a sailor weighs (r276, piece C's research)

Researched 20 Nov 2026 by one researcher run, for the club browser (piece C: each club's racing and character). Real
names here only: the game shows the parody names (src/club/clubs.ts, by the internal id in the first column). Most clubs'
own sites were unreachable from the session, so many rows rest on search snippets and secondary sources (Latitude 38,
California Division of Boating and Waterways listings, directories): check against the clubs' own pages before a fact ships.
"unknown" means not found, not "doesn't exist".

## Folkboat racing

The strongest evidence comes from the repo's own results, src/boat/data/sf-folkboat-results.json. Those results show three hosts:
- GGYC's Manuel Fagundes Seaweed Soup, 2025–26 and 2026–27.
- SFYC's Resin Regatta, 2019 and 2021.
- Sausalito YC's Classic Boat Invitational, spring and fall, 2019 and 2021–2026.

Two more, from search snippets only:
- StFYC's Wednesday Night Woodies on the Cityfront, sponsored by the SF Bay Folkboat Association. The SI PDF is from 2013.
- CYC in Tiburon has hosted the Folkboat International Regatta (2015, 2017 and 2019).

The results' "club" field is the club a boat entered under, so it shows where Folkboat sailors belong, not who hosts. These are rough label counts, not boat counts:
