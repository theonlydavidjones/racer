# The Yacht Club: a joint design (Crew Model and Skipper Model)

Draft for the owner, 12 November 2026. Nothing here is built yet. The mock-ups are in `proposals/yacht-club/`.

## What the owner asked for

> The Yacht Club is the place where crew members are created, configured and assigned to boats, and where the owner sees
> and creates the other skippers and their details. The race setup shows the boat's assigned crew, swappable there, but
> isn't where crew are configured. (ROADMAP, "The Yacht Club", owner's direction, 12 November.)

It is also the home of the club's skill sliders, and later of Seasons' rankings and awards, which become attributes of
the winning boat and skipper (ROADMAP, "Seasons").

## The idea in one paragraph

Today's **Fleet** screen ("Your boats": the grid, the list, each boat's page with her record) becomes the **Yacht Club**,
with three tabs, and a fourth to come:

- **Boats:** today's Fleet. Each boat now shows her skipper and crew, assigned from the roster.
- **Members:** the roster of people, skippers and crew. You create and edit your own here; the club's own members are
  generated.
- **Club:** the sliders that set how good the club's generated members are.
- **Seasons**, later: rankings and awards.

The race setup no longer edits people. It shows each boat's assigned skipper and crew, with a **Swap** that picks from the
roster, plus the seat swap.

## The people

A member is one record, stored with your saves, like your boats (`store/cloud.ts`):

```
Member {
  id, name,
  roles: ['crew'] | ['skipper'] | ['skipper', 'crew'],
  origin: 'yours' | 'club',      // you made them, or the club generated them
  seed,                          // the same person every race
  // as crew (boat/crewPhys.ts Person):
  h, w, skill, attr?: { trim, react, eyes, handling, fitness, composure },
  // as skipper (ai/skipper.ts):
  skipper?: { start, speed, strategy, tactics, rules, consistency, nerve },
  history: their results in your races (places, starts, OCS, penalties), which Seasons builds on
}
```

- **Names are made up**, never a real sailor's (ROADMAP: real skippers' names stay out).
- **You** are a member: the helm of your boat, with your own height, weight and crew attributes. Your skipper attributes
  are you, so there's nothing to set.
- **Your members:** you create them and set everything. A card shows the sliders in plain words:
  - as crew: Trim, Reactions, Eyes, Boat handling, Fitness, Composure;
  - as skipper: Starts, Boat speed, Reading the wind, Tactics, Rules, Consistency, Nerve.
- **Club members:** generated by the Club tab's sliders.
  - **Skippers' ratings are hidden**: the owner chose discovery by racing. A club skipper's card shows what you'd really
    know: name, club and boat, the club gossip line, and her record against you (races, mean place, best, OCS,
    penalties). Later, tendencies seen in her record ("often first off the line").
  - **Crews' ratings are shown.** You can watch a crew work, and knowing them is part of picking yours. (Owner to
    decide.)
  - A **"Show skippers' ratings"** switch in Settings, off by default, for learning and testing. (Owner to decide.)

## Boats and their people

- Each boat (yours and the club's) gets `skipperId` and `crew: memberId[]`, in seat order: middle crew (the main), then
  the forward hand (the jib, the cabin top, the pole). Boats with no assignment today keep working as now: a boat's
  skipper from her name, crews from the club's level.
- **Assigning** happens on the boat's page, under her Particulars: Skipper [pick], Crew [pick] [pick], each picking from
  the roster.
  - One person sails on one boat per race. The picker greys out anyone already aboard another boat in this race.
  - Crew count (0–2) is the number of crew assigned. The class allows 2 or 3 aboard, 4 if three weigh 200 kg or less:
    CR 13.10.
- **Your boat:** you're her skipper. Your crew are your members, or club members you've asked aboard.

## The Club tab: the generator

- Two rows, "Skippers" and "Crews", each with a **Level** (Club night … Worlds) and a **Mix** (Even / Typical / Mixed).
- The club's generated members are drawn from these, each from a fixed seed, so a member is the same race to race.
- Changing the sliders regenerates the club's generated members only. Yours are never touched.
- A boat's crew level follows her skipper's (r ≈ 0.5: in real fleets good skippers sail with good crews).
- The default, "Club night, Typical", is fitted to the real San Francisco fleet's results (Skipper Model's calibration:
  one star boat, a few good ones, a long tail).

## The race setup

The crew rows (today's "Edit crew") become a read-only line per boat:

> **Josephine** · You at the helm · Sam (middle: main) · Alex (forward: jib, pole) [Swap…] [Seats ⇅]

- **Swap** opens the roster picker.
- **Seats ⇅** swaps the two crew.
- "Edit in the Yacht Club" links to the members.
- The fumbles switch stays as it is.
- The competitors' chips show each boat's skipper on hover.

## What's honest about it now

- **Crew attributes** already drive the crew's work: trim, lag, puff calls, pole, hiking and fumbles (phases A–D, on
  branches).
- **Skipper attributes** drive the AI's decisions, but in Skipper Model's calibration results don't yet track skill
  (race-to-race tau about 0.05, against the real fleet's 0.42). The noise is the AI's own fouls, about one penalty per
  boat per race, which Skipper Model is fixing. So a "great" skipper can't yet show it reliably. The Yacht Club can be
  built now; the claim that skill decides races waits on that fix.

## Phases (hours ±50%)

1. **Members and storage:** the record, migrating today's Settings.crew into members (you and your crew), and saves.
   3–4 h.
2. **Members tab:** the roster, the cards, create and edit, the crew and skipper sliders. 4–6 h.
3. **Boats and assignment:** assigning on each boat's page, and the race setup's line with Swap and Seats. Retire the
   setup's crew editor. 3–5 h.
4. **Club tab:** the generator sliders and regenerating, wired to Skipper Model's generator and Crew Model's
   `crewFor(level, mix, seed, skipperDraw)`. The joint calibration test. 3–5 h.
5. **Seasons**, later (ROADMAP: 6–10 h): rankings and awards on members and boats.

Split: Crew Model takes 1, 3 and the crew half of 2; Skipper Model takes the skipper half of 2, and 4. Each reviews the
other's.
