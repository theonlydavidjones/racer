# Marina research, San Francisco, Marin, Richmond and the North Bay (researcher, 20 Nov 2026; r274)

Raw notes from the researcher, in substance. Positions are NOT entrances: NOAA ENC water-body label points from the repo's
src/geo/data/enc-chart.json (cells US5OAKEG/FF/GF/GG/GH/HF/HG), the repo's OSM basin centroids, or Wikipedia points
(flagged); roughly 100–300 m. Blocked hosts: dbw.parks.ca.gov, parks.ca.gov, sausalito.gov, boatingsf.com, nps.gov,
rbra.ca.gov, marinas.com, issuu (facts from them are search snippets only). Main source: NOAA Coast Pilot 7, Ch. 7
(15 Nov 2026 ed.), https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/coast-pilot/files/cp7/CPB7_C07_WEB.pdf (CP7).
(Note: "Olympian YC" and "Brickyard RC" in the repo are the club parody names for the Corinthian YC and Richmond YC.)
