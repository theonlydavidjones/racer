# Marinas: pros and cons as Bay sailors describe them (r274)

This file covers, for each home marina, what sailors and harbor sources say about exposure, surge, current at the
entrance, depth at low water, and getting to the race areas. It adds to the source notes in
docs/marinas/research-west-north.md and docs/marinas/research-east-south.md (branch claude/busy-bohr-fc1mya).

**How it was gathered.** One researcher pass, 20 Nov 2026, kept short because usage is light until Tue 24 Nov. Two
researcher agents ran web searches only.

- **No page was read in full.** latitude38.com, nauticalcharts.noaa.gov (Coast Pilot 7 ch. 7), trawlerforum.com and
  spinnakersailing.com would not resolve from this session. So every claim below is read from a search-result summary.
  Treat each one as "(snippet)" until the page is opened.
- **Labels on each claim:**
  - **sailor**: a sailor or slip holder writing.
  - **school**: a sailing school's guide.
  - **official**: a city, state, federal or harbor document.
  - **listing**: a directory or a third-party app, such as marinas.com or PredictWind.
  - **marketing**: the marina's own words.
- **Our boat's draft is 1.2 m (about 4 ft).** Depth items are read against it.
- **None found** means the pass turned up nothing on that side. It doesn't mean there is nothing to say.

**Distance to the race areas is not given** for any marina (Berkeley Circle, Cityfront, Southampton, Treasure Island).
No source states one, and the pass did not compute any. They can be measured from the game's own marina and course
positions in a later pass, labeled as computed.

## San Francisco

### SF Marina, West Harbor
- Pros:
  - None found.
- Cons:
  - **Shallow slips.** The harbor users' group says the whole West Harbor badly needs dredging. It says this is why the
    harbormaster can't let many of the shallow slips. This bears on a 4 ft draft. (official-ish: users' group,
    https://sfmarinaharbor.org/, undated)
  - **Wave action and silting.** The same group discusses both. A proposed 225 ft sheet-pile breakwater from the Wave
    Organ jetty would protect the slips. That is a proposal, so it shows the concern, not the present state.
    (https://sfmarinaharbor.org/, undated)

### SF Marina, Gashouse Cove (East Harbor)
- Pros:
  - None found.
- Cons:
  - None found specific to the cove. Only the West Harbor's wave and silting notes come close.

### South Beach Harbor
- Pros:
  - **Little current.** PredictWind rates current and tidal flow "Negligible" and maneuverability "Fair". (listing,
    https://predictwind.com/marinas/united-states/california/south-beach-harbor)
  - **Docks and position.** It has concrete docks and a 640 ft guest dock, and sits about 0.5 mi south of the Bay
    Bridge. (marketing, Port of SF, https://sfport.com/maritime/harbors-berthings/south-beach-harbor)
- Cons:
  - **Sea lions** are in the harbor year-round, most active May to August. (official, Port of SF FAQ,
    https://www.sfport.com/maritime/harbors-berthings/south-beach-harbor/south-beach-harbor-faqs)
  - **Waitlist** for berths, longer for larger slips. (official, same FAQ)
  - Surge and entrance: none found.

### Pier 39 Marina
- Pros:
  - None found from sailors.
- Cons:
  - **Sea lions on the docks.** In May 2024 more than 1,000 animals damaged floating docks, and some slip holders were
    moved within the marina. (news, ABC7, May 2024,
    https://abc7news.com/surge-of-sea-lions-swarm-pier-39-san-francisco-experts-say-they-are-following-the-food/14748140/)
    The marina hoses the docks about weekly in warm weather. (marketing, https://pier39.com/?p=1038)
  - Surge and exposure: none found.

## Marin

### Travis Marina, Horseshoe Bay (Fort Baker)
- Pros:
  - **Shelter.** The NPS says the cove's shape gives respite from the Gate's strong winds and currents. (official,
    https://www.nps.gov/goga/planyourvisit/fort-baker.htm)
  - **Position.** It sits right inside the Golden Gate Bridge. (marketing,
    https://slipstreamboating.com/marinas/ca/sausalito/travis-marina/)
- Cons:
  - **Tenure.** A 2020 dispute between the NPS and the Air Force leaseholder put the marina's future in question. The
    arrangement may have changed since. (news, Latitude 38, https://www.latitude38.com/lectronic/2020/02/17/)
  - Depth and current at the entrance: none found.

### Sausalito: the waterfront as a whole
These apply to every Sausalito harbor below:
- **Gusts in fog.** Strong, blustery gusts, especially in fog. (official, Richardson Bay Regional Agency,
  https://www.rbra.ca.gov/caution)
- **Area hazards.** Dense fog, strong afternoon winds and heavy traffic. (guide,
  https://boattest.com/article/boating-guide-san-francisco-ca-marinas-anchorages-and-more)

### Clipper Yacht Harbor (Sausalito)
- Pros:
  - **Protection.** Described as "very good from most directions; only exposed to strong eastern winds". (guide,
    Boattest, URL above)
  - **Size and fuel.** It is the largest Sausalito harbor, with about 700 slips, and has Sausalito's only public fuel
    dock. (official, https://sausalito.gov/our-city/transportation/harbors-and-marinas)
- Cons:
  - **Exposure to the east.** Open to strong easterlies (Boattest, above), plus the Sausalito cautions above.

### Sausalito Yacht Harbor
- Pros:
  - None found beyond its size, 600 berths. (official, sausalito.gov, above)
- Cons:
  - None found beyond the Sausalito cautions above.

### Schoonmaker Point Marina
- Pros:
  - **Amenities.** Called "strictly an upscale marina, with all the amenities". (guide, Northwest Yachting, Jan 2016,
    https://issuu.com/nwyachting/docs/january16/47)
- Cons:
  - **Slip size.** Its slips are for 35 to 70 ft boats (official, sausalito.gov). That it may not suit a Folkboat is our
    inference.
  - **Space.** The same guide advises calling ahead for room.

### Richardson Bay Marina
- Pros:
  - **Slip size.** Its slips are for 26 to 36 ft boats, which fits a Folkboat. (official, sausalito.gov;
    https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/425)
- Cons:
  - None found beyond the Sausalito cautions above.

### Corinthian YC (Tiburon)
- Pros:
  - None found from sailors. It has 94 slips. (official, https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/414)
- Cons:
  - None found. The marinas.com depths read 0.0 ft, which is missing data, so they were not used.

### San Francisco YC (Belvedere)
- Pros:
  - **Position.** It sits on Raccoon Strait, across from Angel Island. (marketing, https://www.sfyc.org/contact-us)
- Cons:
  - None found.

### Paradise Cay
- Pros:
  - **Slip size.** A public harbor with 200 slips for 20 to 40 ft boats. (listing,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/1148_Paradise_Cay_Harbor_Tiburon_CA_United_States)
- Cons:
  - None found. Its listed depths read 0.0 ft, which is missing data.

### San Rafael Yacht Harbor
- Pros:
  - None found.
- Cons:
  - **Too shallow for her.** The harbor's operator said boats drawing more than 24 in. struggle at low tide. Outside the
    harbor it is about 3.5 ft at zero tide. Our boat draws about 47 in. (news, Marin magazine, undated,
    https://marinmagazine.com/community/shallow-waters/amp/)
  - **City warning.** The city warns deep-keel boats to take extra care in the canal at low tide. (official,
    https://cityofsanrafael.org/dredging-to-continue-in-san-rafael-canal-boater-safety-precautions)
  - **Dredging history.** The channel was last fully dredged in 2002 and partly in 2011. A 2022 USACE notice proposed
    more, and it was not confirmed done. (official,
    https://media.defense.gov/2022/Aug/25/2003064926/-1/-1/1/SPN-2022-00294_SANRAFAELYACHTHARBOR_PN_2022.08.26.PDF;
    news, https://www.latitude38.com/lectronic/boaters-plead-for-canal-dredging/)

### Loch Lomond Marina
- Pros:
  - **Basin depth.** Listed at about 6 to 10 ft at mean low water. (guide, Waterway Guide,
    https://waterwayguide.com/reviews/1-5309/safe-harbor-loch-lomond)
- Cons:
  - **Shallow entrance at low tide.** One sailor saw only 5 ft in the entrance channel. Another, drawing 3 ft, churned
    mud leaving. (sailor, marinas.com,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/63c951_Safe_Harbor_Loch_Lomond_San_Rafael_CA_United_States)
  - **A narrow canal with cross current.** The approach canal is dredged to 6 ft MLLW (2002) and is about 30 ft wide.
    It has deceptive shallows outside the markers and "sometimes considerable" cross current. (guide, Waterway Guide,
    above)
  - **Poor marking.** Channel marking is rated "Poor" and current "Beware", and reviewers found the markers hard to see.
    (guide, Waterway Guide, above)

## Richmond

### Richmond YC (Brickyard Cove)
- Pros:
  - **Water and wind.** A Cal Sailing team page says the water is relatively protected and the westerlies consistent.
    (sailor, https://sailing.studentorg.berkeley.edu/about/)
  - **Room to find wind.** The protected water suits small boats, and larger boats find more wind a short way out.
    (news, Latitude 38, Sept 2011, https://issuu.com/bbcdirectories/docs/merging_result_2eeddb447c9fd0/106)
- Cons:
  - None found.

### The Brickyard Marina
- Pros:
  - **Protection.** A reviewer called it "well protected". (sailor, marinas.com,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/7ecmwd_The_Brickyard_Richmond_Marina_Point_Richmond_CA_United_States)
  - **Free of current and surge**, and tucked in from the East Bay chop. (marketing, https://brickyardmarina.com/?p=6897)
- Cons:
  - Mixed service reviews only. Nothing found on water or entrance.

### Marina Bay Yacht Harbor
- Pros:
  - **Deep, and sunny.** "Deep in and out. Lots of sunshine and NO fog." (sailor, marinas.com,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/g9c3mn_Marina_Bay_Yacht_Harbor_Richmond_CA_United_States)
- Cons:
  - **The long channel out.** The only downside named is the trip out the channel to the Bay: "lotta tacks". (sailor,
    same page)
  - **No fuel dock.** (sailor, same page)

## East Bay

### Berkeley Marina
- Pros:
  - **Services.** Fuel, haul-out and repair are listed at 1 Spinnaker Way. (official,
    https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/434)
  - **Slowly improving.** Latitude 38 reports "slow, steady improvements"; only the headline was seen. (news,
    https://www.latitude38.com/lectronic/berkeley-marina-sees-slow-steady-improvements-with-more-on-the-way)
- Cons:
  - **Chop at the entrance.** Typical chop lifts a small boat's outboard clear of the water, so leave fully under sail.
    (school, Modern Sailing orientation guide,
    https://www.modernsailing.com/sites/default/files/Berkeley%20Orientation%20Guide.pdf)
  - **A lee shore in a northerly.** The pier becomes a lee shore, and in a westerly there's a sudden shift off the
    Berkeley YC building in the fairway. (school, same guide)
  - **Shoaling.** The basin was dug from shallow flats and shoals continuously, with depths of −8 to −12 ft MLLW in the
    dredge area. Maintenance dredging to restore −8 ft in the channel was scheduled for Aug–Nov 2025 and not confirmed
    done. (official, Moffatt & Nichol memo for the City, 21 Apr 2021,
    https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2021-04-21%20Draft%20Dredging%20Needs%20Technical%20Memo%20-%20Moffatt%20Nichol.pdf;
    USACE notice, Jan 2024,
    https://media.defense.gov/2024/Jan/24/2003380729/-1/-1/1/SPN-2005-29969%20BERKELEY%20MARINA%20PN%2020240119.PDF;
    https://berkeleyca.gov/your-government/our-work/capital-projects/berkeley-marina-dredging-project)

### Emeryville Marina
- Pros:
  - **Ashore.** Called "an oasis", with plenty of parking. (sailor or guest, marinas.com,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/rnclgp_Safe_Harbor_Emeryville_Emeryville_CA_United_States)
- Cons:
  - None found.

### Emery Cove Yacht Harbor
- Pros:
  - **Size and services.** About 430 slips, with fuel. (official, https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/432)
- Cons:
  - None found.

### The Oakland Estuary: what applies to every Estuary marina
This covers Jack London Square, Embarcadero Cove, Union Point, OYC, Encinal, Marina Village, Fortman, Grand and Alameda
Marina.
- Pros:
  - **Warmer flat water.** The Estuary's courses are "generally warmer, flatwater". (news, Latitude 38, 2025/2026
    Lectronic; the exact page wasn't confirmed)
  - **Racing at the door.** Encinal, Island and Oakland YCs run the shared "Boss of the Estuary" beer-can series, which
    drew about 100 boats. (news, same)
- Cons:
  - **A long beat out to the Bay.** "A lot of upwind tacking against prevailing W winds up the estuary… with lots of wind
    shadows, dead zones and port and ferry traffic." Then there are more wind shadows from Treasure Island, the Bay
    Bridge and downtown SF at the mouth. (sailor, Grand Marina review, marinas.com,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/95cqmj_Grand_Marina_Alameda_CA_United_States)

### Oakland Estuary, marina by marina
Each of these adds only a little to the Estuary notes above.
- **Jack London Square / Embarcadero Cove / Union Point.**
  - A forum poster expected the Estuary to be warmer and less windy than Berkeley. That is a guess, not an observation.
    (sailor, https://www.trawlerforum.com/forums/s28/oakland-area-marinas-29134.html)
  - Union Point's one review is about crime. (marinas.com)
- **Oakland YC.**
  - Members only, with a waitlist, about 200 slips. (listing, https://boatingsf.com/listings/marinas/)
- **Marina Village.**
  - "Protected by the calm waters of the Oakland Estuary", 732 slips. (marketing,
    https://safeharbor.com/locations/safe-harbor-marina-village)
- **Fortman.**
  - "Protected, deep water", 497 slips. (marketing)
- **Grand Marina.**
  - Reasonable prices, and slips available, as of 2010. (sailor, marinas.com, above)
- **Encinal YC and Alameda Marina.**
  - Nothing beyond the Estuary notes.

### Ballena Isle Marina
- Pros:
  - **Protected, and not up the Estuary.** A reviewer called it a "beautiful, protected marina", "not shady like on the
    estuary". (sailor, marinas.com,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/55c38g_Safe_Harbor_Ballena_Isle_Alameda_CA_United_States)
  - Being on the Bay side of Alameda spares the Estuary beat. That is our inference.
- Cons:
  - **Shallow in places.** A 2022 USACE notice shows −5 to −10 ft MLLW in the entrance channel and −3 to −8 ft in the
    basin, with dredging proposed. Check current soundings for a 4 ft draft. (official,
    https://media.defense.gov/2022/Jul/07/2003031799/-1/-1/1/SPN-2022-00242_BALLENA%20ISLE_PN_20220707.PDF)
  - **Old docks.** Some docks are "in need of replacement". (sailor, marinas.com; Dockwa)

### San Leandro Marina
- Pros:
  - None found.
- Cons:
  - **Too shallow for her.** The 2006 USACE survey found an average of 5.7 ft and a minimum of 3 ft, and "little or no
    water" in places at extreme lows. (official, City of San Leandro,
    https://weblink.sanleandro.org/PublicWeblink8/0/doc/188704/Page5.aspx)
  - **The channel.** Latitude 38 calls the channel to the Bay "silted and useless", undated. (news,
    https://www.latitude38.com/lectronic/stick-mud-san-leandro/)

## Peninsula and South Bay

### Oyster Point Marina
- Pros:
  - **Easy approach.** Approaches and channel marking are rated "Excellent". (listing, PredictWind,
    https://explore.predictwind.com/marinas/united-states/california/oyster-point-marina)
- Cons:
  - None found.

### Brisbane Marina (Sierra Point)
- Pros:
  - **Well kept.** A slip holder of about 25 years "would not go anywhere else". (sailor, Dockwa, 2025,
    https://api.dockwa.com/explore/destination/n8c54p-brisbane-marina)
- Cons:
  - None found.

### Coyote Point Marina
- Pros:
  - **Afternoon wind.** Typically 15 to 20 kn, easing toward evening. (listing, PredictWind,
    https://predictwind.com/marinas/united-states/california/coyote-point-marina)
- Cons:
  - **A shallow channel.** It isn't deep enough to approach safely until about halfway between markers 8A and 8.
    (school, Spinnaker Sailing, https://www.spinnakersailing.com/?p=2581)
  - **A dead zone.** The point sits in a wind dead zone, so sail past marker 8 before tacking in. Morning wind often
    fades, so full days often mean motoring. (school, same)
  - **Chop.** It's choppy beyond the rock jetty when it blows. (school, same)

### Redwood City Municipal Marina
- Pros:
  - **Depth.** One listing gives 10 ft at low tide. (listing, https://marinas.com/view/marina/x1cd41)
- Cons:
  - **Conflicting depths.** Another listing gives 7 ft minimum in the channel. No sailor comment found.

### Westpoint Harbor
- Pros:
  - **Dredged.** The marina says it is dredged, with 8 ft at MLW on the approach, and that boats drawing 7 ft or more
    sail from it. (marketing, marinas.com responses,
    https://marinas.com/view/marina/x1cleyn_Westpoint_Harbor_Redwood_City_CA_United_States)
- Cons:
  - **Shallow at the guest dock.** A visitor measured about 2 ft there at a −1.15 ft tide, and warned drafts over 3 ft
    on minus tides. That was one dock on one tide. (sailor, same page)

## Treasure Island

### Treasure Isle Marina and Clipper Cove
- Pros:
  - **Flat water.** Protected on three sides, with flat water in any wind but east. (guide, https://boatingsf.com/san-francisco-bay-anchorages/;
    Bay Crossings, 2008, https://www.baycrossings.com/?p=10738)
  - **Depth and holding.** About 15 ft across most of the anchorage (Latitude 38, Apr 2021,
    https://issuu.com/latitude38/docs/l38202104/64), and good holding (BCDC letter, 2025,
    https://www.bcdc.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/354/2024/12/2025-02-06-clipper-cove-Treasure-Island-Marina-letters.pdf).
- Cons:
  - **Open to the east.** Exposed in strong easterlies. One sailor's log found no wind protection mid-cove, which
    conflicts with the above. (sailor, SeaPeople,
    https://dashboard.seapeopleapp.com/trip/ee4ef83d-689b-4382-98bc-224e7c9a516b)
  - **Rules.** It is a no-wake zone, 5 mph. Anchoring more than 24 h needs a free TIDA permit, up to 96 h. (official,
    https://sf.gov/information/anchor-your-recreational-vessel-clipper-cove)
  - **The anchorage's future.** A proposed marina plan may remove it. (BCDC letter, above)
