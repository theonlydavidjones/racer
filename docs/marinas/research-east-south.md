# Marina research, East Bay and South Bay (researcher, 20 Nov 2026; r274)

Raw notes from the researcher, kept as returned in substance. Positions: only Coast Pilot 7's Redwood City fix and the repo's
OSM basin centroids; no NOAA chart/ENC entrance fixes yet. Several hosts refused fetches (dbw.parks.ca.gov, berkeleyca.gov,
smcgov.org, westpointharbor.com, emerycove.com); citations marked "snippet" were read only as search snippets.
Main source read in full: NOAA Coast Pilot 7, Ch. 7 (15 Nov 2026 ed.), https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/coast-pilot/files/cp7/CPB7_C07_WEB.pdf (CP7).
