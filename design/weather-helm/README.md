# Weather helm in the tiller guide (r035): mock-up, nothing built

The owner's purpose: "to help players learn about and judge weather helm." Inside the tiller guide (shown while you hold
the tiller), no new button or strip, a picture and not words:

- the **cursor bar** (orange, as now): where the tiller is this instant;
- a **soft white pill**: the tiller's average over the last few seconds, which is her weather helm when she's sailing straight;
- a **faint outlined band** 2–5° to windward: the well-balanced helm sailors cite. It sits on the windward side, so it
  swaps sides with the tack.
- The degrees ("4° weather helm") show only on hover over the pill.

Sources (rules of thumb, not a textbook): Good Old Boat, "the rest of the ratios on helm balance": "between 2 and 4
degrees of weather helm for optimal performance upwind"; "above 8 degrees ... too much weather helm". A sailing forum
gives 3–5 as ideal and about 10 as too much. Nothing specific to the Folkboat was found. The band is an ESTIMATE.

on screen to the right. Port tack: mirrored. Made by `mockup.cjs`, which adds the marks to the page by hand at 4° average
and 6.5° now.
