The start sequence

Class flag (F, Folkboats)FLYING
The warning signal: up with one sound at the start of the sequence, down with one sound at the start. Your class is next.
P
The preparatory signal: up a minute after the warning, down with one long sound at one minute to go. The usual start: if you’re over at the gun, come back across the line and start (individual recall, Rule 29.1).
I
Rule 30.1: if you’re on the course side of the line or its extensions in the last minute, you must sail back around an end of the line to start.
Z
Rule 30.2: if you’re in the triangle between the line ends and the first mark in the last minute, you get a 20% scoring penalty.
U
Rule 30.3: if you’re in the triangle between the line ends and the first mark in the last minute, you’re disqualified.
Black flag
Rule 30.4: if you’re in the triangle between the line ends and the first mark in the last minute, you’re disqualified, even after a general recall.

What changes

  1. A clear column for the rig. The middle 40% of the screen, from the top edge down to the sail controls band, holds nothing fixed. Only labels on the world itself go there (boat names, marks).
  2. Instruments move to the top corners. Left: start clock, boat speed (with heading), and the round compass. Right: wind and current, then pause, speed, sound and exit as small icons.
  3. The compass is a dial like the others, top left: her heading in big numbers over a short tape, the true wind's direction in blue, the next mark's bearing in yellow. The windex is round, in the band between the two vangs, where the mast is as you look forward: a yellow arrowhead into the apparent wind and a fin for the tail, white tabs at close-hauled, a solid red wedge between them for too close to the wind, and the apparent wind angle underneath.
  4. One card, top left, always answers "where next, and how far?" Start line before the gun; Over early (red) until you've gone back; Next mark after the start (name, side to leave it, distance, a sketch of the rounding; the compass's yellow triangle points at the same mark); Finish line on the last leg; the Navigator's next waypoint when cruising. Try them with the buttons above the picture.
  5. The start-line banners become that card before the start in the left column: your distance to the line, a sketch of the line with the favoured end starred and you as a dot (the arrow is which way you're heading), and one line of words. The transit hint moves to its hover text and the messages.
  6. The chase camera starts with the whole boat between the top strip and the band: masthead about an eighth of the way down, transom just above the band. You can zoom in past that; the top of the mast then goes off screen.
  7. The trim panel goes. The sail lines move into the band. The coach's one line, heel, helm and the crew become a small Coach card bottom left.
  8. Bottom left: things you do (course; the pole is in the band). Bottom right: the crew and the view. Tapping Crew opens the crew's places, as the crew row does today.
  9. The chart inset is round, top right under the wind and current.
  10. The Flags tile shows only the flags flying. Click it for the flag index: every flag the race committee flies in the sim, when it goes up and what it means, and the numeral pennants. The ones flying now are marked.
  11. Hail pops up when it's needed, as an amber tab on the band's top-left corner, saying what you'd call ("Starboard!", "Room to tack!", or the reply "You tack"). Click it or press H. It's gone when no hail applies.
  12. Messages move to a card bottom right, level with the Coach card: the latest two, with All for the full log (L).

Decided

  1. Zooming in: allowed past the clear space; the top of the mast goes off screen.
  2. The round compass right of the band; messages in a card bottom right, level with the Coach card.
  3. The windex stays as it is today.
