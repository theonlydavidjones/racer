# Learn to sail against ASA 101 (Debrief, 13 Nov 2026)

The source is ASA 101 Keelboat Sailing 1, *Standards* (PDF, read 13 Nov 2026):
https://americansailing.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Certification-ASA-101-Standards.pdf. It has no prerequisites, and covers a 20–27 ft sloop "in light to
moderate conditions, with winds up to 15 knots". It's examined by a written test (the knowledge items 1–20) and an
on-the-water practical (the skill items 21–40).

## What changes with ASA

- **Points of sail:** ASA 101 asks for five (S28–32). Lesson 4 now has five. Done.
- **The knowledge cards (owner's decision needed).** The approved cards came from US Sailing BK: its 4 knots, distress
  signals and first aid. ASA 101's list differs:
  - **Knots (S40):** six, not four: figure-8, clove hitch, cleat hitch, reef (square) knot, round turn & two half hitches, bowline.
  - **Safety (K16–20):** federally required equipment, navigation lights, the float plan, reporting accidents, the BAC limit.
    First aid isn't in ASA 101. Distress signals come in only as part of the required equipment.
  - Proposal: follow ASA, with six knot cards and five safety cards.
- **"Jibe" or "gybe" (owner's decision needed):** ASA writes "jibe", which is also the usual US spelling. The sim says "gybe"
  everywhere (the drill, the debrief, the keys). The US-spelling rule would make it "jibe" everywhere, including the
  commands ("Ready to jibe" … "Jibe-ho"). This is one sweep with scripts/check/spelling.cjs.
- **The badge:** "101 · Basic Keelboat skills". Its hover is "Practice of the ASA 101 skills in the sim. Not the ASA
  certification." Lessons 8 and 11 aren't needed for it.
