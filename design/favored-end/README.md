# 8za: finding the favored end yourself (mock)

The owner: "start practice also needs to help me learn how to pick the favored side. don't just tell me."

Stills over the real page (mock.cjs; the overlays are drawn in the page, not built).

1. **Read it** (`8za-1-read.jpg`, overhead): head to wind near the line. Two lines are drawn on the water:
   - her bow's line (amber);
   - the line's square (white, dashed);
   - with the angle between them (18°).
   The line's ends are named where the line leaves the view. Her bow leans toward the end that's favored.
2. **Call it** (`8za-2-called.jpg`, the start-line card): "Which end? ◀ GGYC flagpole · X ▶", in the sketch's own order.
   - Until the gun nothing tells you: no "… END FAVORED", no ★, no amber end marker in the sketch.
   - The lane runs to the line's middle, not the favored third.
   - The call is a click here, or the two arrow keys.
3. **Scored** (`8za-3-debrief.jpg`): the debrief's first line is the call: "Your call: X ✓ · favored 18°".
   - It has a small picture: the line, its square, and the wind leaning by the bias.
   - The dots (●●○) are the help fading: the water lines are drawn for the first three calls, then you read it alone.

Not drawn here, the other two ways sailors read it (proposed, both visual):
- **The compass:** a white tick on the heading tape at the line's square heading, beside the wind's blue one; the gap is the bias.
- **Sighting down the line:** from either end, in the cockpit view.
